# Korean Service Disclosures

Read this reference only when creating, reviewing, or revising a Korean-facing service draft that may handle personal data, create accounts, sell directly to consumers, or use recurring billing. This is a design and content-quality checklist, not legal advice. Follow applicable law, approved legal copy, and project-specific requirements.

- Do not remove, hide, or dismiss relevant business identity, terms, privacy, subscription, cancellation, refund, or other service disclosures as visual clutter without confirming they are out of scope.
- Identify early whether the service handles personal data, uses account creation, sells directly to consumers, or includes recurring billing. Build the applicable disclosures and navigation paths into the information architecture rather than adding them as a final visual patch.
- When required business or policy details are unavailable for an explicitly conceptual draft, use clearly labeled placeholders or a handoff checklist. Do not invent company details, registration numbers, policy terms, prices, refund rules, or legal-compliance claims. Do not ship empty or misleading disclosure links.
- For consumer-facing paid subscriptions, make the service-provider identity, price and billing cycle, automatic-renewal terms, cancellation and refund path, customer support, and terms easy to find before purchase and from the footer. Do not apply consumer-sales disclosures automatically to a documented B2B-only or free service.
- During verification, confirm that applicable disclosures remain available and that any concept placeholders are clearly labeled and non-deceptive.
